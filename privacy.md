# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Notice for unglazed ("**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("**process**") your information when you use our services ("**Services**"), including when you:

  * Download and use our mobile application Lightning TOPIK (the "**App**") on iPhone or Android, including its widgets, or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice

  * Visit our website at [lightning-topik.com](https://lightning-topik.com) (the "**Site**")

  * Engage with us in other related ways, including any support conversations

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [ung@sai-korean.com](mailto:ung@sai-korean.com).

## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

**What personal information do we process?** Almost none. Lightning TOPIK has no accounts and no sign-up. The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location from your device's location services, and the iPhone App does not collect Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). We record usage events tied to an app-generated install identifier that is not linked to your identity — see Section 1. On Android, the App also shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ads software collects the information it needs to serve them: your device's Android advertising ID, your IP address (from which Google may estimate your general location) and other device and ad-interaction information — see Section 2.

**Where is my study progress?** On your phone. Your words, reviews, plan, exam date, and settings are stored in the App's storage on your iPhone or Android phone, which the App's widget also reads. We run no server for the App and have no copy of your progress. If you use your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google's backup on Android), it may include your study progress; it is kept with Apple or Google, we cannot see it, and a reinstall or a new phone may restore your progress from it.

**Do we use tracking or ads?** The iPhone App contains no advertising and no cross-app or cross-site trackers. It does not collect IDFA and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The Android App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob until you buy Remove ads; where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), it asks for your consent before the first ad — see Section 2. Both apps use Google Analytics for Firebase for product analytics (we read aggregated reports); Google, through Firebase Analytics, may also receive this data under Firebase's data-sharing settings — Section 3 sets out who receives what.

**What about the website?** The Site has no accounts and sets no cookies. Cloudflare delivers it, and we count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which stores nothing on your device and does not identify you — see Section 1.

**How do I pay?** On iPhone, the Lifetime unlock is an Apple in-app purchase: Apple handles payment, and the App never receives your payment details or Apple ID. On Android, Remove ads is a Google Play in-app purchase: Google handles payment, and the App never receives your payment details or Google account; Google Play gives us order records only. Both apps record purchases with RevenueCat, a service that stores purchase records under a random install ID the App generates, not under your name or account — see Section 1.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete the App: that removes your study progress from your phone. If you also back up your phone, that backup follows your backup settings with Apple or Google; delete it or turn it off in your iCloud or Google account settings. To have RevenueCat's purchase records for your install deleted, email us — see Section 6.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE PROCESS?

**Information you provide.** Lightning TOPIK requires no account and no registration. The App does not ask for, and does not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location from your device's location services. The iPhone App collects no advertising identifier. On Android, the ads software in the App collects your device's advertising ID and IP address, as described in Section 2.

**Information stored on your device.** Your study data — the TOPIK level you choose, the words you study and how you rate them, review schedules, saved words, your study plan and exam date, reminder times, and settings — is stored in the App's private storage on your device. The App's widget (on iPhone, the lock screen widget; on Android, the Home screen widget) reads the same storage to show today's word. This data is not sent to us. If you use your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google's backup on Android), the backup may include this data. It is kept with Apple or Google under your account, we cannot see it, and a reinstall or a new phone may restore your progress from it.

Apart from your phone's own backup and the online voice described under Device features, the only information that leaves your device is:

  * **Usage events.** The App reports a small set of events to Google Analytics for Firebase — for example which onboarding steps are viewed, when a study session ends and how many cards it had, how a card was rated (Again, Hard, Good or Easy), the TOPIK level and daily pace chosen for a plan, and when the purchase screen is opened or a purchase completes (on Android, Google Analytics may also record the purchased product's ID, name and price automatically). Event details are fixed labels and counts, never text you type. These are sent with an app-generated install identifier (Firebase's app instance ID), device model, OS and app version, preferred language, and an approximate country or region that Google derives from your IP address (the App does not read your location; on iPhone, Apple's vendor identifier (IDFV) is not collected, and on Android, Google Analytics does not collect the advertising ID: both collections are switched off in the App). They contain nothing that identifies you personally. Deleting and reinstalling the App normally resets these identifiers. Google, through Firebase Analytics, may also receive these events and the information sent with them under Firebase's data-sharing settings, as described in Section 3.
  * **Purchase information.** If you buy the Lifetime unlock on iPhone, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; if you buy Remove ads on Android, payment is handled entirely by Google through Google Play. The App checks your purchase status with Apple or Google on your device. The App does not receive your name, Apple ID, Google account, or payment details; on Android, Google Play gives us order records only. Both apps use RevenueCat, which records the purchase: the App sends RevenueCat a random install ID that it generates and stores on your device (in the iPhone Keychain; on Android, in Block Store and the App's storage, so it usually survives a reinstall on the same device), together with the purchase records of in-app purchases (product, price, currency, store country, dates and the store's transaction IDs), the App version, platform, your device's OS version and model, and the IP address of the connection. This sends no advertising identifier (no IDFA, IDFV or Android advertising ID), name, email, Apple ID, Google account, payment details or study data. Apple and Google also send RevenueCat notices of purchases and refunds of the App's in-app products, with the same install ID attached to the purchase. We use it to see sales, refunds and conversion reports per store and country; it is not used for advertising or tracking.
  * **Ads on Android.** The information Google's ads software collects to show ads, described in Section 2.
  * **Support email.** If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to include in your message.

**Device features.** On iPhone, word pronunciation is spoken by Apple's on-device speech synthesis. On Android, it is spoken by the text-to-speech engine set on your device (for example, Google's Speech Services); if that engine uses an online voice, the word being spoken is sent to the engine's provider to be read aloud. The App does not use the microphone or record audio. Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you allow notifications; we run no push notification server.

**Our website.** The Site has no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and it sets no cookies. Cloudflare delivers it: as with any website, your browser sends your IP address, the page you ask for, the page that referred you and your browser's user agent, and Cloudflare uses them to deliver the page and to protect the Site from attacks; we see only aggregated traffic reports. Each page also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, a small script that reports the page you viewed, the page that referred you, your browser, operating system and device type, an approximate country, and how fast the page loaded. It stores nothing on your device (no cookies or local storage) and does not fingerprint or identify you; we see only aggregated counts. The Site's store buttons link to the App Store and Google Play with a label for the page they are on, so Apple and Google can report to us, in aggregate, how many installs came from the Site.

## 2. THE ANDROID APP: ADS, CONSENT AND GOOGLE PLAY

This section applies only to the Android App. The iPhone App shows no ads.

**Ads.** The Android App is free and shows native ads from Google AdMob in three places: on the Home tab below your study card, in a small unit above the word card during a review, and on the page after a session. It shows no ads during onboarding or on your first day, and none after you buy Remove ads. We limit ads to content suitable for everyone (AdMob's "G" rating), matching the App's own rating.

**What Google's ads software collects.** To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App automatically collects and sends to Google:

  * your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  * device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device;
  * how you interact with ads and the App (for example app launches, ad views and taps); and
  * diagnostic information about the performance of the App and the ads software.

Google uses this information as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads; the path depends on your device); after you delete it, ads can no longer be personalized with it.

**Your consent.** Where the law requires consent for ads (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App shows Google's consent message, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform, before the first ad. It never appears during onboarding. Your choices are stored on your device in the standard format of the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework, and you can change them at any time in Profile › Privacy choices. If you decline, you may still see ads that are not personalized, as Google allows. Where your consent is required, Google Analytics for Firebase's analytics storage stays off until you give it; until then it may still send Google usage events without device or user identifiers, which Google uses for modeling. We do not use Firebase for advertising: its ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization settings are switched off. For users whose device is set up in the United States, ads in the App use Google's restricted data processing.

**Remove ads.** Remove ads is a one-time Google Play in-app purchase. Google handles the payment; the App asks Google Play for the price and whether you own it, whenever you open the App, so it can turn the ads off. After you buy it, the App stops loading ads and the ads software no longer starts.

**Permissions.** The Android App, and the Google software built into it, declare Android permissions in these groups: notifications, and restoring study reminders after your phone restarts; internet access and network status, which the ads, analytics and purchase software need; Google Play purchases (billing); keeping the phone awake while background work runs; and, for the ads and analytics software, advertising and measurement permissions: the advertising ID, Android's ad services, the Google Play install referrer, and basic phone state, which lets the ads software read basic, non-sensitive phone information such as the type of mobile network, never your phone number, calls or contacts. The App does not ask for permission to read your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, files, calls or messages.

## 3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE INFORMATION?

We share information only with the companies that provide the services the App and the Site need:

  * **Google LLC** — provides Google Analytics for Firebase, which processes the usage events described in Section 1, and, in the Android App, Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform described in Section 2, and Google Play's purchases. Through Firebase Analytics, Google may also receive these usage events and the information sent with them for its own purposes, such as improving its products and services, under Firebase's data-sharing settings, which we have left as they are. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  * **Apple Inc.** — processes App Store purchases and provides the on-device frameworks the iPhone App uses (speech, notifications, widgets). See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
  * **RevenueCat, Inc.** (United States) — records the purchases made in the App, as described under Purchase information in Section 1: the random install ID, purchase records, app, platform and device information, and IP address. It processes them on our behalf, so that we can see sales, refunds and conversion reports. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
  * **Cloudflare, Inc.** — hosts and delivers the Site and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics, described under Our website in Section 1. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Who receives what: Google receives the usage events described in Section 1, which it may use for its own purposes as described above, and, in the Android App, the ads information described in Section 2. Apple, or Google on Android, processes the purchase you make, and RevenueCat receives its record, as described above. Cloudflare processes visits to the Site. We use neither your study data nor your usage events for advertising or cross-app tracking. We may disclose information if required by law, but as described above, we hold effectively no information about you to disclose, beyond what you email us and, on Android, the order records Google Play gives us, and the purchase reports we see in RevenueCat.

## 4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP INFORMATION?

  * **Usage events:** kept by Google Analytics for Firebase under its data retention settings, after which Google deletes them automatically. Aggregated reports may persist beyond that, and data Google receives under Firebase's data-sharing settings is kept under Google's own policies.
  * **Purchase records:** kept by RevenueCat, under the random install ID, under its own retention policies. Deleting the App does not delete them; the ID is random and not linked to your identity.
  * **Ads information (Android):** kept by Google under its own retention policies; we receive only aggregated ad reports.
  * **On your device:** your study data, and on Android your ad consent choices, are kept until you delete the App. A backup of them made by your phone is kept with Apple or Google under your backup settings.
  * **Website visits:** Cloudflare Web Analytics reports cover the previous six months; Cloudflare keeps its records of requests to the Site under its own policies. We see only aggregated reports.
  * **Support email:** kept as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

## 5. HOW DO WE KEEP INFORMATION SAFE?

Your study data stays on your device, protected by its operating system; any backup of it that your phone makes is held and protected by Apple or Google. Usage events and ads information are sent to Google, and purchase records to RevenueCat, over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We have implemented appropriate technical measures designed to protect the limited information we process. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 6. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on where you live (for example, the Republic of Korea, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability of your personal information, and the right to withdraw consent.

Because Lightning TOPIK keeps your study data on your device, with no study data on our side, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App, which removes it from your phone. A backup of it that your phone made follows your backup settings: delete it or turn it off in your iCloud or Google account settings. On Android, where the App asked for your consent, you can withdraw or change it in Profile › Privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. To have RevenueCat's purchase records for your install deleted, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them; deleting the App alone does not do this. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us at [ung@sai-korean.com](mailto:ung@sai-korean.com) and we will respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## 7. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

Our Services are not directed to children under 14 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no accounts and no social features. The iPhone App shows no advertising; the Android App, which shows ads, is listed on Google Play for users aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through the support email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Google Analytics for Firebase, and in the Android App Google's ads services, may process information on servers located in the United States or other countries. RevenueCat is based in the United States and processes purchase records there or in other countries. Cloudflare delivers the Site from data centers around the world and may process visit information in the United States or other countries.

Yes, we will update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Notice. We encourage you to review this Privacy Notice from time to time.
