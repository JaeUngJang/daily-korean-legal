# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated October 18, 2026**

This Privacy Notice for unglazed ("**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("**process**") your information when you use our services ("**Services**"), including when you:

  * Download and use our mobile application Lightning TOPIK (the "**App**"), including its lock screen widget, or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice

  * Engage with us in other related ways, including any support conversations

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [jjw3435@gmail.com](mailto:jjw3435@gmail.com).

## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

**What personal information do we process?** Almost none. Lightning TOPIK has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). We record anonymous usage events tied to an app-generated install identifier that is not linked to your identity — see Section 1.

**Where is my study progress?** On your device only. Your words, reviews, plan, exam date, and settings are stored in the App's storage on your iPhone, which the App's lock screen widget also reads. We run no server for the App and have no copy of your progress.

**Do we use tracking or ads?** The App contains no advertising and no cross-app or cross-site trackers, and we never sell or share your information for advertising. The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous, aggregated product analytics. It does not collect IDFA and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

**How do I pay?** The Lifetime unlock is an Apple in-app purchase. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details or Apple ID.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete the App. Because your progress lives only on your device, deleting the App deletes it completely.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE PROCESS?

**Information you provide.** Lightning TOPIK requires no account and no registration. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.

**Information stored on your device.** Your study data — the TOPIK level you choose, the words you study and how you rate them, review schedules, saved words, your study plan and exam date, reminder times, and settings — is stored in the App's private storage on your device. The App's lock screen widget reads the same storage to show today's word. This data is not sent to us.

The only information that leaves your device is:

  * **Anonymous usage events.** The App reports a small set of events to Google Analytics for Firebase — for example which onboarding steps are viewed, when a study session ends and how many cards it had, how a card was rated (Again, Hard, Good or Easy), the TOPIK level and daily pace chosen for a plan, and when the purchase screen is opened or a purchase completes. Event details are fixed labels and counts, never text you type. These are sent with an app-generated install identifier (Firebase's app instance ID), your device's vendor identifier (IDFV), device model, OS and app version, preferred language, and an approximate country or region derived from your IP address. They contain nothing that identifies you personally. Deleting and reinstalling the App resets these identifiers.
  * **Purchase information.** If you buy the Lifetime unlock, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock the App. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details.
  * **Support email.** If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to include in your message.

**Device features.** Word pronunciation is spoken by Apple's on-device speech synthesis; the App does not use the microphone or record audio. Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you allow notifications; we run no push notification server.

## 2. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE INFORMATION?

We share information only with the service providers required to run the App:

  * **Google LLC** — provides Google Analytics for Firebase, which processes the anonymous usage events described in Section 1. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  * **Apple Inc.** — processes App Store purchases and provides the on-device frameworks the App uses (speech, notifications, widgets). See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising or cross-app tracking. We may disclose information if required by law, but as described above, we hold effectively no information about you to disclose.

## 3. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP INFORMATION?

  * **Usage events:** kept by Google Analytics for Firebase under its data retention settings, after which Google deletes them automatically. Aggregated reports may persist beyond that.
  * **On your device:** your study data is kept until you delete the App.
  * **Support email:** kept as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

## 4. HOW DO WE KEEP INFORMATION SAFE?

Your study data stays on your device, protected by iOS. Usage events are sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We have implemented appropriate technical measures designed to protect the limited information we process. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 5. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on where you live (for example, the Republic of Korea, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability of your personal information.

Because Lightning TOPIK keeps your study data only on your device, with no data on our side linked to you, you can exercise deletion directly at any time by deleting the App. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us at [jjw3435@gmail.com](mailto:jjw3435@gmail.com) and we will respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## 6. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

Our Services are not directed to children under 14 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App has no accounts, no social features, and no advertising. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through the support email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Google Analytics for Firebase may process usage events on servers located in the United States or other countries.

Yes, we will update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Notice. We encourage you to review this Privacy Notice from time to time.
